About

A little about me.

Software engineer who loves building systems that scale — from config-driven SaaS platforms to machine learning tools.

I'm Hritik Suman, a Senior Software Engineer currently at Algebrik AI — an AI-powered Loan Origination System that transforms the loan lifecycle through automation and smarter decisions.

With over 5 years of experience across AI startups, fintech, and enterprise consulting, I specialize in Angular, full-stack development, and building scalable platform architectures. My most significant work at InCred Financial Services was designing a Config Driven UI / Stateless SaaS platform that enabled complete UI customization without touching code.

I also have hands-on experience in data visualization from my time at Deloitte, where I built real-time charting applications for Caterpillar Inc. using Plotly.js.

I began my career as a software engineering intern and worked my way up, picking up machine learning, backend systems, and cloud deployments along the way. I graduated with a BTech in Computer Science from JIIT Noida in 2021.

Outside of work: competitive programming, ML experiments, and reading about system design.
